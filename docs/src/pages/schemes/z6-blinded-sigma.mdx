# Z6 · Blinded Σ-Protocol & Instance Helpers

**Status: implemented** (`src/sigma::z2`, `src/instance::ring`,
`src/foundation::sampling`) — the ZK blinding step for the
committed-opening line, plus the instance-layer helpers the folding
ecosystem builds on.

## Blinded HVZK Σ-response over the Z2 ring (`sigma::z2`)

Knowledge of a short opening `w` (`‖w‖∞ ≤ B_W`) of the commitment
`c = A·w`, **without revealing `w`**:

```text
y ← centered-bounded (‖y‖∞ < B_Y)
d = A·y                    ──c, d──▶  C ← small non-unit challenge
z = y + C·w                ──z──▶     1. A·z == d + C·c   (exact)
                                      2. ‖z‖∞ ≤ B_Z       (HVZK gate)
```

The challenge resolves the 2-adic dilemma: small challenges in
`Z_{2^k}[X]/(X^N+1)` are otherwise always units (odd coefficient sum =
invertible), which breaks extraction — while the large `X − a` challenge
blows up the response norm (`‖C‖₁ ≈ 2^31`). `nonunit_small_poly` draws
ternary `{-1, 0, 1}` coefficients conditioned on an **even** sum:
`‖C‖₁ ≤ N` *and* non-invertible. Soundness is relaxed forking extraction
(`A·(z1 − z2) = (d1 − d2) + (C1 − C2)·c`); HVZK comes from the
rejection-sampled response distribution.

## Integer lift (`instance::ring::split_mod_2k`)

The LaZer lift turns the modular statement `A·s ≡ t (mod 2^k)` into the
exact ring equation `A·s + 2^k·v = t` with a hidden short carry vector
`v` — no arithmetic mod a non-ring modulus.

## 2-adic ModSwitch (`instance::ring::modswitch_ring`)

Coefficient truncation `Z_{2^32}[X]/(X^D+1) → Z_Q[X]/(X^D+1)` is a ring
homomorphism: `ms(A·w) == ms(A)·ms(w)` — commitments re-stated over
smaller rings without re-opening the witness (the switching step the
Alpine/Shadow line builds on).

## Strong-sampling-set challenges (`foundation::sampling::odd_sum_poly`)

Uniform draws conditioned on an odd coefficient sum — the LatticeFold
challenge-set notion where differences stay invertible (odd sums are
units of the 2-adic ring). The small-non-unit counterpart is
`nonunit_small_poly` (even sum, ternary).
